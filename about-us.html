OUR PHILOSOPHY

We believe luxury should feel effortless.

At Sorèva, we believe luxury is a feeling—not a price tag. We created Sorèva with a simple vision: to make beautifully crafted, AAA-grade cubic zirconia jewellery with a genuine diamond-inspired sparkle accessible to every woman who loves elegance, confidence, and timeless style.

A NOTE FROM OUR FOUNDERS

Crafting Jewellery That Becomes A Natural Part Of You.

“When we founded Sorèva, we asked ourselves why extraordinary jewellery had to be locked away in vaults, saved only for rarest milestones, while daily wear was dominated by fast fashion that tarnished within weeks.

We envisioned a different standard: demi-fine quiet luxury. Pieces designed with 24K gold plating, impeccably faceted AAA-grade cubic zirconia, and lightweight hypoallergenic ergonomics that transition effortlessly from high-stakes morning meetings to radiant evening celebrations.

Every Sorèva piece represents our promise to you: uncompromising quality, accessible indulgence, and personal elegance that shines with you every single day.”

— Sonal Belchada & Shruthi Shivakumar The House of Sorèva
THE SORÈVA VISION

Quiet luxury, beautifully reimagined.

Our designs follow the quiet-luxury movement—clean lines, lightweight settings, and understated sparkle that layer effortlessly from your morning meeting to your evening plans. We believe jewellery doesn't need to be loud to make an impression. The most beautiful pieces are often the ones that become a natural part of who you are.
