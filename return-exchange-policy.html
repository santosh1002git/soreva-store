Transparent, Fair, and Rigorous Return Parameters

At Sorèva, each jewellery creation undergoes comprehensive quality inspection before beginning its journey to you. To ensure fairness, hygiene, and the integrity of our pieces, claims are strictly governed by transparent verification parameters.

01

Our Quality Promise

Every piece deserves to arrive beautifully and impeccably presented.

Eligible Situations

Claims are strictly considered when an order arrives damaged during transit, an incorrect item/variant is sent, or a verified manufacturing flaw is discovered upon opening.

Transit Damage Incorrect Item Manufacturing Flaw

Quality Controlled

Every creation is meticulously checked prior to dispatch under strict hygiene, stone-setting durability, and demi-fine luxury standards to eliminate defects.

Multi-Point Check 24K Gold Plated Hallmarked Finish

Non-Returnable Situations

Claims are not accepted for change of mind, subtle color variations due to monitor screens, or damage caused by improper customer handling, moisture, or wear.

No Change of Mind Screen Nuances Mishandling Excluded
02

Claims Process

How to initiate a replacement or claim with our customer support desk.

STEP 01

Contact Us Promptly

Please contact our customer support desk within 48 hours of delivery if you believe your order qualifies for a replacement or return.

STEP 02

Record Your Unboxing

Provide a clear and continuous unboxing video showing the outer parcel condition, shipping label, seal opening, and the jewellery piece inside.

STEP 03

Share Photographs

Submit high-definition photographs clearly displaying the parcel's condition and any visible issue or defect with the piece for verification.

IMPORTANT TIME REQUIREMENT

48 Hours Matter

Claims must be submitted to our customer support desk within 48 hours of delivery. Please retain all original packaging, certificates, and tags, and ensure your unboxing video and photographs clearly document the condition of the package and product upon arrival.

04

Refund & Exchange Timeline

Swift, transparent processing once your claim is verified.

INSPECTION & SETTLEMENT

Guaranteed Source Refund

Once our quality verification team receives and inspects the returned product and verifies the supporting claim documentation, your replacement piece or refund is initiated promptly.

Refunds are credited back directly to the original payment source (Credit/Debit Card, Net Banking, or UPI) without unnecessary delays or hidden deductions.

Settlement Window 5–7 Business Days
Original Source 100% Value Fast Processing
COMMON CLARIFICATIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Because all our jewellery pieces are inspected and hand-packaged under high-resolution cameras prior to dispatch, an uncut, continuous unboxing video provides undeniable proof of the parcel's condition as handed over by the courier, safeguarding both you and our artisans.
Due to strict logistics courier claim deadlines and the delicate nature of demi-fine jewellery, claims submitted past the 48-hour window cannot be accepted for refunds or exchanges. We urge you to inspect your parcel promptly upon arrival.
To maintain uncompromising hygiene and pristine quality for all patrons, Sorèva does not offer exchanges or refunds for personal changes of mind or personal styling preferences. Please consult our detailed dimensions, photos, and sizing guides prior to purchasing.
Approved refunds are credited directly back to the original method of payment (UPI, Credit/Debit Card, or Net Banking) within 5–7 business days following parcel arrival and inspection at our quality center.
