International Shipping Charges

International shipping charges vary depending on the destination, parcel size, jewellery selected and volumetric weight.
For international air shipments, courier charges are generally calculated using the volumetric weight of the package, which includes the jewellery packaging and outer shipping materials.

REGION 1

USA, Canada & Europe

Starts from approx. USD 32

For larger orders, shipping may typically be calculated at approximately 6–8% of the order value, depending on the destination and parcel characteristics.

Illustrative Example:
Order Value: USD 1,000
Estimated Shipping: approx. USD 60–80
REGION 2

Africa, Asia & Australia

Starts from approx. USD 42

For larger orders, shipping may typically range around 9–12% of the order value, depending on the destination, parcel characteristics and courier rates.

Illustrative Example:
Order Value: USD 1,000
Estimated Shipping: approx. USD 90–120

Important Note on Shipping Charges

The above amounts are indicative estimates only and are provided to give you an idea of international shipping costs. Actual shipping charges may vary based on:

Destination country and postal code
Jewellery selected
Number of pieces in the order
Package dimensions & volumetric weight
Courier rates applicable at time of shipment
Customs & local delivery requirements
The final shipping charge applicable to your order will be confirmed at checkout or before dispatch.

Customs, Duties & Import Taxes

International orders may be subject to customs duties, import taxes or other local charges depending on the destination country.

Any such charges, where applicable, are determined by the destination country's customs authorities and may be payable by the customer.

Sorèva and our courier partners will provide the necessary shipping documentation and coordinate the customs clearance process wherever required.
