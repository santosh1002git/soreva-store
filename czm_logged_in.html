      More Product Details

      Availability In-Stock. Available for immediate Worldwide Delivery.
      Back Chain Details This Necklace contains a Back Chain which can fit in all neck sizes. Extensions ensures that it fits beautifully in all neck sizes.
      Metal High Quality Brass is the base metal of this Jewelry Set. Brass is an alloy of Copper, Zinc and certain other metals. The quality of Brass depends on the proportion in which metals are used. An ideal proportion ensures malleability as well as durability.
      Interesting Facts Each of our Necklace Sets have to go through at least 28 different types of Craftsmen! Starting from the Jewellery Designer, till the Final Polishing Specialist !
      Gemstone Setting Process You will be surprised to know that there are thousands of Gemstones in the Necklace Set and each and every tiny gemstone is hand set securely with prongs on all 4 sides. This prevents the gemstones from falling off.
      Plating Process Electroplating is done with generous amount of Gold and Rhodium.
      Size Info This Necklace Set has been designed in such a flexible way that it will easily fit in any Neck size.
      Jewelry Care Do not spray perfume on the Necklace directly. After every wear, please rub the necklace with a cotton cloth and store in a box. Avoid keeping Jewelry in Velvet Boxes. Remember, sweat and perfumes are the worst enemies of any Jewelry.
