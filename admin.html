Sorèva Master Image Size & Resolution Guide

🧒

The 3-Year-Old Golden Rule (Only 3 Shapes to Remember!)

1. Perfect Square (1000 × 1000 px)

Use for Products, Categories & Instagram. 1:1 shape always fits.

2. Widescreen TV (1920 × 1080 px)

Use for Hero Top Carousel Banners. 16:9 full-bleed landscape.

3. Wide Strip (800 × 200 px)

Use for Brand Logos. 4:1 wide strip with transparent PNG.

30-Second Recipe For Anyone On Your Team

  1. Resize: Open free Canva.com or Photopea.com $\rightarrow$ Set custom size (e.g. 1000 × 1000 for products, 1920 × 1080 for banners).
  2. Save: Download as JPG (Quality: 85%) or PNG (for transparent logos).
  3. Compress: Drop into TinyPNG.com to shrink file size under 300 KB without losing sharpness.
  4. Paste Link: Enter URL in the admin field. The live inspector automatically validates the resolution!
